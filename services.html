Yes. For international patrons in North America, Europe, and the Middle East, we ship our calibrated Calico Test Kit and conduct live high-definition virtual fitting appointments with Aria Vance. We also offer private in-suite fittings during Milan Fashion Week.
Unlike conventional industrial silk where cocoons are boiled, Ahimsa silk allows the Bombyx mori silkworm to complete its metamorphosis and fly away as a moth. The fibers yield a richer organic matte luster, breathability, and lifelong tensile strength.
Yes, our master embroidery guild specializes in carefully reinforcing antique Kanjivaram and Benarasi bridal textiles, weaving them into contemporary architectural silhouettes while protecting ancestral bullion wire work.
